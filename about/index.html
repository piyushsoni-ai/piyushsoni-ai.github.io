About

SEO foundations. AI search thinking. Practical execution.

Piyush Kumar Soni, SEO & AI Search Leader. Working in search and web technologies since 2011.

I started working across digital, web, and search technologies in 2011. My career has evolved from hands-on SEO and website work into technical SEO, organic growth strategy, team leadership, digital project delivery, AI Search, LLM workflows, RAG, and automation.

My core strength is connecting traditional search fundamentals with newer AI-driven discovery.

I work across areas such as crawlability, indexation, site architecture, structured data, internal linking, search intent, topical authority, content optimization, entity understanding, GEO, AEO, AI visibility, and automation.

Alongside strategy, I enjoy building practical tools and workflows that turn ideas into working systems. Some are public, like SEO Master Skill and the GBP Audit + AI Visibility extension. Others, like SnapLife AI, are products I am building on my own time.

My approach

Strong SEO fundamentals + structured information + authority + entity clarity + useful content + AI-ready systems.

AI Search has not replaced SEO. It has raised the cost of weak foundations. A page that is hard to crawl, vague about who is behind it, or thin on evidence will struggle in classic results and in AI answers alike. So I fix the foundations first, then build the clarity and authority that both kinds of search reward.

GBP Audit + AI Visibility

A Firefox extension designed to audit Google Business Profiles for local SEO and AI visibility signals.

Get Firefox extension (opens in a new tab)Read the case study

Problem

Google Business Profile audits often require checking many signals manually: categories, description, review recency, response rate, updates, services. Most audit tools also ignore a newer question clients now ask: does the business show up when someone asks ChatGPT or Gemini?

Solution

I wanted a faster way to review profile strength while also bringing AI visibility considerations into the workflow. You open a business in Google Maps or Search, click the sidebar icon, and get a scored report with prioritized fixes and a PDF you can send.

My role

Built it end to end: audit logic, scoring model, extension code and report design. Published under Lucky Digitals.

Why I built it

Google Business Profile audits often require checking many signals manually. I wanted a faster way to review profile strength while also bringing AI visibility considerations into the workflow.

Capabilities

  • GBP Health Score from 0 to 100 across 8 sections: completeness, categories, description, reviews, review recency, response rate, business updates and services
  • Review analysis: rating, recency, response rate and star distribution
  • AI Visibility Score showing how often the business appears in ChatGPT and Gemini answers
  • Rule-based recommendations ranked by priority
  • PDF report export, generated in the browser
  • Input by Maps URL, GBP URL, short link, or business name and country

Privacy and limits

The audit runs in the browser. There is no login, no account, no tracking and no analytics. A fair-use free tier covers 5 GBP audits and 25 AI checks per device, which keeps the paid AI-answer lookups sustainable.

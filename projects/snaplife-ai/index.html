SnapLife AI

Your daily life assistant, powered by photos. Snap. Decide. Save.

Read the case study

Problem

Small daily decisions eat time: what to cook with what is in the fridge, what to wear, where the money went. Separate apps exist for each, but each asks you to type everything in.

Solution

One app with three modes, each driven by a photo. The AI reads the image, combines it with what the app already knows about you, and returns a decision you can act on.

My role

Product owner and developer. I am designing the product, data model and AI pipeline and building it myself.

Three modes

Eat

Snap your fridge and receive recipe suggestions based on available ingredients.

Wear

Photograph your wardrobe and receive outfit suggestions.

Track

Scan receipts and organize spending information.

Tech stack

Frontend

Backend

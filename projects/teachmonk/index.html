TeachMonk

Bilingual CBT practice platform for competitive exams, in English and Hindi.

Problem

Students preparing for competitive exams in India need realistic computer-based test practice, and many of them prefer Hindi. Most practice platforms treat Hindi as an afterthought: partial translations, mixed-language pages and weak search visibility for Hindi queries.

Solution

TeachMonk is being designed as a scalable English and Hindi CBT practice platform supporting mock tests, practice sets, topic tests, sectional tests, previous-paper practice, and performance analysis. Both languages are first-class from day one.

My role

Product owner, architect and SEO lead.

Bilingual architecture

English lives at teachmonk.in/ and Hindi at teachmonk.in/hi/. Each language gets its own crawlable URLs, hreflang pairs and native-language metadata, so Hindi pages can rank for Hindi searches instead of being hidden behind a toggle.

Focus areas

  • Bilingual architecture
  • CBT engine that mirrors real exam timing and navigation
  • Exam configuration per exam pattern
  • Question bank with topic tagging
  • Admin panel for content and tests
  • SEO architecture for exam, topic and test pages
  • Performance analytics for students
  • PWA-first approach for low-bandwidth mobile users

Status

TeachMonk is under development and has not launched publicly. This page describes the product plan and architecture.

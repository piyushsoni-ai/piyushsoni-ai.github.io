Case study

211K clicks, 10.5M impressions & growing AI Search visibility

A search growth project combining Technical SEO, content strategy, internal linking, authority development, structured data, and AI Search visibility work.

Challenge

The goal was to grow organic search traffic for a site competing in a crowded international market, and to make sure the brand was found and described correctly as more searches moved into AI answers. The client is not named here because the work is covered by confidentiality.

Diagnosis

I started with the parts of the site search engines see first and worked outward:

  • Technical SEO: crawl paths, indexation, canonicals, redirects and rendering
  • Content gaps: topics with clear demand and no strong page
  • Architecture: how sections and templates grouped related content
  • Internal linking: whether links pointed at the pages that mattered
  • Search intent: whether each target page matched what searchers wanted
  • Authority: the link and mention profile against competitors
  • AI Search visibility: where the brand appeared, or did not, in AI-generated answers

Strategy

Fix the foundations before scaling content. Once crawling and indexation were clean, build topic clusters around the strongest commercial and informational themes, link them deliberately, and make every key page easy for both search engines and language models to understand: clear entities, direct answers and accurate structured data.

Execution

  • Technical fixes across crawlability, indexation and canonical signals
  • Information architecture and topic cluster planning
  • New and refreshed content mapped to intent
  • Internal linking from hubs to supporting pages and back
  • Structured data for key templates
  • Authority development through relevant links and mentions
  • Tracking of AI Overview and AI answer appearances alongside rankings

Results

  • 211Kclicks
  • 10.5Mimpressions
  • 80Kclicks in 28 days
  • 66.3Kestimated organic traffic
  • 9.6K+ranking keywords
  • 206AI responses
  • 36AI Overviews

Lessons

No single tactic produced the growth. The technical clean-up made the content work count, internal linking concentrated authority where it was needed, and the same clarity that helps a page rank also made it easier to cite in AI answers. Treating AI visibility as an extension of good SEO, rather than a separate project, kept the work focused.

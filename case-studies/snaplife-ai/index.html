Designing SnapLife AI: one photo, three everyday decisions

SnapLife AI started from a simple observation: a photo carries most of the information people otherwise type into three different apps. This is how the product and architecture are being shaped.

Product idea

Three daily decisions, one input. Photograph the fridge and get recipes. Photograph the wardrobe and get outfits. Photograph a receipt and see spending organized. The value grows over time, because the app remembers your wardrobe, what you cook and where your money goes.

Multimodal input

Every mode starts with an image. A vision-capable model extracts structured data, such as ingredients, clothing items with colour and type, or receipt line items. That structured data, not the raw image, drives suggestions. This keeps prompts small and makes results easier to check.

AI architecture and model fallback

Gemini 2.0 Flash is the primary model because it handles images quickly at low cost. Groq running Llama 4 Scout is the fallback when the primary is slow, rate-limited or unavailable. Suggestions should never fail just because one provider has a bad hour.

Database

Supabase provides PostgreSQL, auth, storage and edge functions in one place. pgvector stores embeddings so the app can find similar wardrobe items or recipes a user has liked before. Plan limits, credits and pricing live in a settings table, so they can change from an admin dashboard without a code release.

PWA first

The first release is a Next.js 14 progressive web app with the App Router and Tailwind CSS. A PWA can be installed on a phone, opens the camera directly and avoids app store delays while the product is still being shaped. The code lives in a pnpm and Turborepo monorepo so a native app can share the same backend later.

Privacy considerations

Wardrobe mode can use a body photo for fit suggestions, and that needs consent. Users choose to store the photo or have it scanned once and discarded, or they can pick a body type without uploading anything. Receipts and fridge photos are handled with the same principle: keep only what the feature needs.

Product decisions

  • One app with three modes, instead of three apps
  • Free plan with monthly credits, plus a single Pro plan
  • Payments through Razorpay for Indian users
  • Admin-editable pricing and limits

Status

SnapLife AI is a private project in development. The source repository is private and the product has not launched.

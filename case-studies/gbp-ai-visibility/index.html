Case study

Building a GBP + AI visibility audit workflow

Local SEO audits were taking too long and missing a question clients had started asking: do we show up in AI answers? This is how the workflow became a Firefox extension.

Problem

A proper Google Business Profile review means checking a long list of signals by hand, writing them up, and turning them into a priority list. It takes time per profile, and two people auditing the same profile often reach different conclusions. Clients had also started asking whether ChatGPT or Gemini recommend them, and the usual GBP tools do not answer that.

Research

I went through the signals that consistently separate strong local profiles from weak ones and grouped them into eight areas: completeness, categories, description, reviews, review recency, response rate, business updates and services. Each one is visible on the profile itself, so the audit can run from the page the auditor is already looking at.

Audit logic

Each area gets a score, and the eight scores roll up into a GBP Health Score from 0 to 100. Review data gets its own analysis: rating, recency, response rate and star distribution. A rule-based engine then turns weak scores into recommendations ranked by priority. Rules instead of free-form AI output means the same profile always gets the same advice, which matters when the report goes to a client.

Why a browser extension

Auditors already work inside Google Maps and Search. An extension removes the copy-paste step: open the business, click the sidebar icon, read the report. It also lets the audit run locally in the browser with no login, account, tracking or analytics.

Local SEO meets AI visibility

The AI Visibility Score shows how often the business appears in ChatGPT and Gemini answers, using DataForSEO's LLM scrapers. Seeing a strong GBP score next to a weak AI score is often the most useful finding in the report, because it shows that profile work alone is not enough.

Report workflow

The report exports to PDF directly in the browser, ready to send. Inputs are flexible: a Maps URL, a GBP URL, a short link, or a business name and country. A fair-use free tier of 5 GBP audits and 25 AI checks per device keeps the paid lookups under control.

What I would do next

Collect feedback from real audits, tune the scoring weights, and add more answer engines to the AI visibility check as reliable data sources become available.

About

From SEO to AI, one agency, one skillset at a time

I am an SEO Expert and AI/LLM Specialist at Lucky Digitals in Kanpur, with 15+ years of hands-on digital experience spanning SEO, web development, digital marketing, team leadership and automation.

My SEO background covers technical SEO, on-page and off-page optimization, schema markup, local SEO, analytics and search growth. More recently, I have expanded that work into Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), AI search optimization, Entity SEO and SEO automation so brands can improve visibility across both traditional search and AI-driven discovery.

I build LLM-powered tools, AI agents, RAG workflows and automation pipelines using technologies such as the OpenAI API, LangChain, Python and Hugging Face. The focus is practical: applying AI to SEO reporting, content workflows, structured data, research, client operations and digital growth.

My advantage is the overlap between SEO strategy, technical implementation and AI/LLM development. I approach AI search as an extension of strong entity, technical and content foundations rather than as a replacement for SEO.
